NeuroSenSing

Harnessing Digital Phenotyping for Remote Monitoring of Cognition in Early-Stage Alzheimer's Disease

Thank you for joining the NeuroSenSing study. Your participation helps advance cognitive health monitoring with wearable and smartphone data.

Guidelines

To support the success of the study and data quality, please follow these guidelines:

  • Wear your Fitbit during daily activities and sleep.
  • Keep your phone's location services enabled.
  • Complete the questionnaire and cognitive assessment at the start and end of the study.
  • Sync the NeuroSenSing and Fitbit apps at least every five days and keep them running in the background.
